SM Workload Net

Work arrives scattered across email, Teams, Jira, Freshservice and Confluence, and some of it never becomes a ticket. Each teammate has a board here showing the work that was detected but has nothing tracking it — so nobody has to hunt five systems to find out what they are missing.

Open gaps

Totals across every claimed seat. A gap is work the scan found evidence of that has no CS issue, no Freshservice ticket, no change record, or no current documentation behind it.

The team

one board each

Each teammate owns one file. Editing your own board never touches anyone else’s, so two people can push at the same time without a conflict.

How the net works

4 layers

The layers run in order — nothing reaches a board until it has been read, cross-referenced, and found to be missing a record.

01

Connect

Read access into the places work starts: Microsoft 365 mail, calendar and Teams, Jira, Confluence, Freshservice and SharePoint.

02

Watch & reason

One artifact triages mail into candidate work items. Another cross-references those against Jira CS and the Freshservice System Management queue to find work that is real but untracked.

03

Act on a cadence

A frequent pass flags anything urgent. Twice a week a deeper pass drafts the suggested CS issues, the change requests that belong at CMB, and the Confluence pages that have drifted.

04

Land on a board

Each pass rewrites the board-data block in one teammate’s page and pushes. GitHub Actions republishes the site within a minute.

Claim your seat

3 steps

Do this once. It is also the exercise — your first branch, your first commit, your first look at a rendered diff.

01

Copy the template

Duplicate people/_seat.html and rename it to your GitHub handle, e.g. people/jdoe.html.

02

Fill in your block

Edit the board-data JSON at the bottom of your new file: your name, handle, coverage, and the three Claude pages you run.

03

Take a seat

In index.html, replace one empty {} in the seats list with your entry, then commit. Your card appears on this page.
